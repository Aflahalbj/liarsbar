Liar's Table - credits

Gameplay rules are adapted from Liar's Bar (Curve Animation, 2024). No models, textures,
sounds, or music from Liar's Bar are included in this mod.

Bundled libraries
- Veil (FoundryMC) - post-processing and dynamic lights
- Player Animator (KosmX) - MIT
- bendy-lib (KosmX) - MIT

Required/optional mods (not bundled)
- GeckoLib
- Simple Voice Chat (optional)

Assets (every asset must be self-made or CC0 and listed here with its source)
- textures/entity/table_top.png, table_side.png, white.png:
  self-made, generated procedurally by tools/make_textures.py.
- textures/card/*.png: self-made 64x96 pixel art, generated by tools/make_cards.py
  (hand-drawn shapes and hand-made pixel glyphs, no fonts).
- Bots use Minecraft's own built-in default player skins (not shipped with this mod).
- geo/item/revolver.geo.json, textures/item/revolver.png: self-made, generated by tools/make_revolver.py.
- textures/fx/smoke.png, muzzle_flash.png, blood_splat*.png, blood_pool.png, textures/entity/lamp_shade.png,
  chair_wood.png, chair_leather.png: self-made (tools/make_textures.py).
- sounds/*.ogg (heartbeat, revolver mechanics, gunshot, tinnitus, breaths, body fall): self-made, synthesized
  from scratch with numpy/scipy by tools/make_sounds.py. No recorded samples are used.
- sounds/card_*.ogg (riffle, slide, place, shove, fan): recorded foley from "Casino Audio" 1.1 by Kenney
  (www.kenney.nl), CC0 1.0. Converted to mono; card_riffle* are cut from card-shuffle.ogg.
- Bar room ambience (rain on the windows, distant thunder) reuses Minecraft's own built-in sounds
  (weather.rain, entity.lightning_bolt.thunder); nothing is shipped with this mod.
- player_animation/*.json, cinematic/roulette.json: self-made, generated by tools/table_anims.py.
- Veil light-visibility CPU fallback (mixin/veil/IndirectLightRendererMixin) follows the approach in the
  user's own Backrooms mod.
- textures/block/** (bar set blocks: parquet, damask wallpaper, wainscot, pressed tin, brick, stone, counter,
  cabinet, bottles, mirror, rainy night windows, sconce, stool, fan, pipe, cut-paper posters, frosted door, sign,
  awning): self-made pixel art, drawn by tools/make_blocks.py (designed palettes/patterns, no random noise).
  The poster revolver silhouette reuses our own docs/artbible/revolver_profile.txt.
- models/block/**, blockstates/**: self-made, generated by tools/make_block_models.py.
- textures/item/whiskey_glass.png, whiskey_bottle.png: self-made pixel art (tools/make_blocks.py).
- player_animation/npc_*.json (bar NPC drinking, talking, laughing, wiping, fetching, pouring): self-made,
  generated by tools/npc_anims.py. Bar NPCs wear Minecraft's own built-in default skins.
- data/liarsbar/structures/bar.nbt: self-made, built from tools/bar_structure.mjs with minecraft-builder-skill
  (mattzh72, Apache-2.0; build-time tool only, nothing from it is shipped). Uses vanilla barrels, blackstone stairs, deepslate paving and lanterns.
